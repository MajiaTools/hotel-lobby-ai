## What it does

- **2 photos (left + right performer)** → a duet performance at one microphone in a warm hotel lobby scene
- ~**11s** MP4, 768p, built-in scene reference (no video upload needed)
- Works with **friends, couples, pets, or a person + a pet** — one subject per photo, clear faces, good light
- Optional custom prompt direction (mood, expression, small performance detail); the template keeps the movement, scene and left/right roles
- Confirmed failed renders return your credits

1. Open https://hotellobbyvideo.online
2. Upload two clear photos (JPG/PNG/WebP, up to 10 MB each, at least 300×300 px)
3. Set their places (first photo left, second photo right), generate, and find the MP4 in My videos
